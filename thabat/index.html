ThabatDiscipline for imperfect days

BETA

Thabat (ثبات, "steadiness") helps you keep your daily routines going when the day does not go to plan. A smaller version of a routine still counts, a hard day can be excused, and a blank day is never shamed. Everything you write stays on your phone. Thabat is free, for iPhone and Android, and made by one independent developer.
