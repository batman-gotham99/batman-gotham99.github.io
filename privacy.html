Thabat

Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026 · Applies to the Thabat app for iPhone and Android

In short. Your routines, logs and notes stay on your phone. There is no account and no Thabat server. If you leave "Share anonymous usage" on, the app sends anonymous usage counts and crash reports so we can fix and improve it. You can turn that off at any time.

Who we are

Thabat is made by one person, an independent developer, and is in beta. In this policy, "we" means that developer.

Contact: batman.gothann@gmail.com

What stays on your phone

Thabat keeps everything you enter in a database on your phone:

  • your routines, their versions and reminder times;
  • what you log each day, and the reasons you tap in Can't today;
  • notes, sketches and todos;
  • your weekly reviews;
  • feedback you write, and any screenshots you attach to it;
  • your settings, including the name you give the app.

We cannot see any of it. It never leaves your phone unless you export a copy or send feedback yourself.

Export and import. Settings → Data → Export a copy saves a file and opens your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes. Import reads a file you pick. We never receive these files.

Excused days

You can mark days as excused, for example when you are ill or travelling. The app never records why. There is no field for the reason, so there is nothing to store or send.

What is sent when "Share anonymous usage" is on

This switch is in Settings → Data → Share anonymous usage. During the beta it starts on. When it is on, two kinds of data leave your phone.

1. Anonymous usage events, sent to PostHog (EU region). Twelve kinds of events, such as "a routine was logged", "a day closed" or "a note was written". They tell us which features help. Each event carries:

  • a random install id made on your phone (not your phone's own id, and not an advertising id);
  • for events about a routine, a random key for that routine, never its name;
  • the platform (iPhone or Android), the app build, the beta or release stage, your language setting and text direction, and any test group;
  • general device details the analytics library adds, such as the phone model, system version and screen size.

Settings → About → What we collect lists all twelve events in plain words.

2. Crash reports, sent to Sentry (EU region). When the app fails, a report says what went wrong: the error, the stack trace, the app build, the phone model and system version. Before a report leaves your phone, routine names, checklist parts, notes, todos, feedback text and your name are replaced with placeholders. A report never includes screenshots.

Never sent

  • routine names, version or part names, notes, sketches, todos, or feedback text;
  • amounts or counts you log;
  • your name, email address, contacts or location;
  • why a day was excused;
  • anything at all about a routine started from a faith starting point (such as prayers or Quran reading). Those events are dropped on your phone before anything is sent.

We ask both services not to keep your IP address and not to look up your location. We do not use this data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to track you across other apps or websites. PostHog and Sentry process it for us, under our instructions, as our service providers. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

How long we keep it. Usage events for up to 12 months, crash reports for up to 90 days. Then they are deleted.

How to turn it off

Go to Settings → Data → Share anonymous usage and turn it off. The app then sends no usage events and no crash reports. It also throws away the random install id and routine keys and makes new ones, so nothing sent before can be linked to anything sent later.

Feedback

Settings → Send feedback (or a shake) prepares an email to our address. It opens in your own mail app, and you can read and change it before you send it. Nothing is sent until you tap Send there.

The email carries only what you type and the screenshots you choose to attach. If "Include my day's details" is on, it also adds the date, the time of day, the app version and build, the screen you were on, and any recent "couldn't save" errors. It never adds routine names, notes or amounts.

We see your email address because you write to us from it. We use feedback only to improve Thabat and to reply to you. A copy stays on your phone; you can remove its screenshots in Settings → Data → Remove feedback screenshots.

Attaching a screenshot asks for access to your photos only at that moment. The app never uses the camera or the microphone.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications. Your phone schedules and shows them. No notification server is involved, and no push token is sent to us.

App updates

Small app updates are downloaded from Expo, the platform the app is built on, in the same way as any download: the request says which app version and platform need the update. It carries none of your data.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: Settings → Data → Erase everything removes your routines, logs, notes, todos, feedback and settings. Uninstalling the app also removes everything it stored.
  • Feedback emails: write to us and we will delete them.
  • Usage events and crash reports: turning sharing off cuts the link to everything sent before. You can also write to us and ask us to delete them. Because the data is anonymous, we may need details such as the platform and the dates you used the app to find it.

We answer requests within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to its use, or to complain to a data-protection authority. Most of your data never reaches us. For what does, email us and we will help.

Children

Thabat is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has sent us data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date before the change applies. If a change affects what leaves your phone, we will also say so in the app.
